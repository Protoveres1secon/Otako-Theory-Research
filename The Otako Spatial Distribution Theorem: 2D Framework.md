The Otako Theorem: O-Ta Measurement System (International Version)Formula 1: 2D Calculation of (O)Viewing O-ta from a top-down perspective.O-ta is bounded within a $3 \times 3$ square grid.Grid cell arrangement (Rows / Columns):Col 1Col 2Col 3123456789Formula 2: The Law of DistributionEach row or column follows a structural ratio of 1-2-1.The $1-2-1$ ratio is a geometric law, not an arithmetic operation.Example (Analyzing the column with cells 1, 4, 7):Cell 1 $\approx$ 1/2 part.Cell 4 $\approx$ nearly 2 parts (approximately $1.9$).Cell 7 $\approx$ 1/2 part.Formula 3: Area Measurement ($S_i$)Let $S_i$ be the area of "O" located within the $i$-th cell.Consequently (for cells 1, 4, and 7):$$S_1 : S_4 : S_7 \approx 1 : 2 : 1$$(Note: This represents the ideal conceptual ratio; practical reality is an approximation).
