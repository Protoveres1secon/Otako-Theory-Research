O-Ta LAWS

Law I: (First-Direction Law)

Compact form:
| n(d) => n * a → b |

Full form:
| n(d) => n * a → n * b |

Law II: Inaccessibility

n(d) ∉ → c

Law III: Infinite Doubling Law

If multiple O-Ta structures are joined together,
there will be 1 chum that cannot be entered.

Law IV: Structural Conservation
(stability)

- small
- nested
- connected
- expanded
- enlarged

→ does not eliminate Laws I and II

Law V: External Force

When an external object intervenes,
the system will no longer be completely constrained by O-Ta.
