O-Ta-Ko Theory
Part VII. Fundamental Axioms
Axiom 1 — Unified Dual-Domain Structure

The O-Ta-Ko System consists of two geometric domains, d and c, which are linked together as one unified structure.

Binh and Chum are not independent structures; they are domains within the unified O-Ta-Ko structure.

Axiom 2 — Dual-Terminal Structure

The O-Ta-Ko System possesses exactly two endpoints: a and b.

No third endpoint exists within the fundamental O-Ta-Ko structure.

Axiom 3 — Fundamental Trajectory

n(d) → n*a → b

An entity belonging to Binh Domain d follows the fundamental relation from its interaction with Entry Point a toward Terminal Point b.

Axiom 4 — Inaccessibility

n(d) ⇒ n ∉⇒ c

An entity n belonging to Binh Domain d is inaccessible to Chum Domain c in the absence of an external force.

Axiom 5 — Dynamic Domain

Chum Domain c exists, but it is not a path of traversal.

c is a dynamic domain, rather than a route through which an entity can simply travel.
